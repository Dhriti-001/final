Application Overview

  • Dashboard: Quick glance at your scores, progress and recent activity.
  • Drills: Practice specific attack‑vectors with timed challenges.
  • AI Inspector: Ask the AI for hints, explanations or deeper analysis.
  • Audit Lab: Step‑by‑step security audit with theory, evidence and questions.
  • Performance & Certificate: View detailed performance metrics and download your certificate.

Common FAQs

How do I reset my password?
Click the Profile link, then select Change Password. Follow the on‑screen instructions.
Why is my score not updating?
Scores are saved after each answer submission. If the page is cached, refresh (F5) to see the latest values.
Can I retry a completed audit stage?
Yes – return to the stage via the Audit Lab navigation and click “Retake”. All previous answers will be overwritten.

Troubleshooting

  • Ensure JavaScript is enabled in your browser.
  • If a page fails to load, clear the browser cache or try an incognito window.
  • For server‑side errors, check the console output where the Flask app is running.
  • Contact support at 1930 (National Cyber Helpline) for critical issues.
